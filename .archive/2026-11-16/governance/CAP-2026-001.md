## Proposed Change

### Principle I (proposed)

> **I. Product Identity Is Fixed (NON-NEGOTIABLE)**
>
> RITE means Rotary Inter-country Teacher Exchange, a West Wichita Rotary / Rotary District 5680
> program that builds awareness of an active teacher exchange begun in 1996, connects the people
> who make it possible, and helps them learn from one another.

**Rationale**: Principle I's wording was implicated by the same 2026-11-15 review as Principle II
(both frame what RITE fundamentally *is*); folding the ratified awareness-first language into
Principle I's identity statement keeps the two principles consistent rather than amending only one.

### Principle II (proposed)

> **II. The Product Builds Awareness, Connection, and Shared Learning (NON-NEGOTIABLE)**
>
> RITE builds awareness of an active teacher exchange, connects the people who make it possible,
> and helps them learn from one another. Core scope includes the history timeline, story archive,
> alumni directory, school and club profiles, community registration, resource sharing, and future
> participant workflows — now understood as serving that awareness-and-connection purpose rather
> than as a "living archive" in itself.

**Rationale**: Applies the 2026-11-15 ratified mission statement verbatim as the principle's
opening sentence, removes the resolved pending-amendment note, and keeps the existing scope list
(history, stories, directory, schools, registration, resources, participant workflows) unchanged —
none of that scope is in dispute, only the framing around it.

**MUST Requirements:**

- Every reference to RITE's purpose in durable documentation must describe it as building
  awareness, connection, and shared learning about an active program, not as a "living archive."
- The scope list in Principle II (history, stories, directory, schools, registration, resources,
  participant workflows) remains unchanged; this amendment is a framing change, not a scope change.

**SHOULD Recommendations:**

- Future copy changes (site content, stakeholder materials) should adopt the ratified wording
  verbatim where practical, rather than paraphrasing it differently each time.
